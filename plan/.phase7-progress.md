## Safety rules

- Unknown execution outcomes require reconciliation before resubmission.
- Reconciliation must not mutate an order into a terminal state from an unrecognized provider status.
- Account/environment mismatches fail closed.
- Provider credentials and SDK DTOs remain inside the adapter.
- Orphan provider orders are reported only; they are never auto-adopted or resubmitted.
- Missing provider orders are reported only; automatic resubmission remains outside reconciliation.
- Lifecycle events are descriptive and do not authorize adoption, resubmission, or provider mutation.
- Recovery replay is idempotent by deterministic event identity and never authorizes provider mutation.
- CI remains informational/non-blocking; market holidays/closed sessions do not stop implementation progression.
