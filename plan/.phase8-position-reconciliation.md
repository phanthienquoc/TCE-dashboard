## Objective

Establish authoritative, restart-safe position truth for downstream dividend lifecycle and automatic take-profit execution.

## Implemented sub-slice — provider-neutral core

- `libs/tce/src/reconciliation/position-state-reconciler.ts` defines the local/provider position contract and a pure deterministic comparison.
- The core normalizes symbols, checks quantities and average prices, and explicitly reports converged, local-only, provider-only, mismatched, ambiguous and invalid states.
- It does not auto-adopt provider-only holdings, auto-close local-only holdings, persist changes or submit orders.
- Exit-data completeness requires an authoritative provider market price and sellable quantity plus local target, slot and pool metadata.
- Recovery now fails closed on missing snapshots, invalid timestamps, invalid positions and duplicate normalized-symbol ownership rather than silently returning an empty account or dropping positions.
